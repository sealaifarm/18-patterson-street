New homes, carefully built

18 Patterson Street brings two new three-bedroom residences to Dorchester. Each home spans three stories and is finished with fiber cement siding, a covered entry, and energy-efficient windows.

Inside, the plans call for hardwood stairs, tiled bathrooms, solid surface kitchen countertops, and laundry on the upper floors, with off-street parking on site.
