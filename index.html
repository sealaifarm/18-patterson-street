18 Patterson Street

A New Chapter for Patterson Street

18 Patterson brings two new residences to a residential corner of Dorchester. Each home offers three bedrooms, two and a half baths, and more than 1,603 square feet across three stories.

The exterior features durable fiber cement siding, a covered entry, and energy-efficient windows. Inside, wide-plank hardwood floors run throughout, complemented by hardwood stairs, Italian marble tiling, ornate bathroom tilework, and solid-surface kitchen countertops, bringing together practical details and a clean, contemporary aesthetic. Laundry is located on the upper floors, with off-street parking on site.

Designed by one of Dorchester’s premier designers, the residences bring a fresh, modern feel to Patterson Street.
