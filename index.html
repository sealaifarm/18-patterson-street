18 Patterson Street

New homes, carefully built

18 Patterson Street brings two new three-bedroom, three-bath residences to Dorchester, Massachusetts. Each home offers 1,603+ square feet across three stories and is finished with fiber cement siding, a covered entry, and energy-efficient windows.

Inside, the plans call for hardwood stairs, tiled bathrooms, solid surface kitchen countertops, and laundry on the upper floors, with off-street parking on site. The corner lot at Patterson and Brookvale Streets was designed by 686 Architects.
